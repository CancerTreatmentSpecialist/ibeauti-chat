Before you chat

The iBeauti Advisor is an AI assistant for beauty, skincare and cosmetic questions, and for comparing prices and availability near you.

  • Prices and inventory change often. Always confirm with the retailer before you go.
  • Not medical advice. For rashes, allergic reactions, persistent acne or any skin concern, see a dermatologist or healthcare provider.
  • Patch test new products, especially if you have sensitive skin or known allergies.
  • Protect your privacy. Don't share passwords, payment details or other sensitive personal information in the chat. A ZIP code is all the advisor needs to shop locally.
  • Conversations are processed by Microsoft Copilot Studio to generate responses.
